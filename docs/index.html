GAME RULES

Steampunks Plane Two

Launch. Collect. Land on the train.

1–10

Number symbols

Numbers add their displayed value to the aircraft's current multiplier.

×2 – ×10

Multiplier symbols

These multiply the aircraft's current multiplier by the displayed amount.

Mechanical pigeons

Mechanical pigeons halve your current multiplier, rounded to the nearest 0.1× with a 0.1× minimum. A collision also knocks the aircraft downwards and leaves a smoke trail.

How to play

  • Choose a bet, then press play. Every flight starts at 1×.
  • The aircraft flies automatically. Pickups give it lift; mechanical pigeons push it down.
  • Land on a glowing train wagon to win the displayed amount. A ground crash pays zero.
  • The maximum payout is 250× your bet.
  • The amount above the aircraft is its current value in FUN credits.
  • The pilot boards before takeoff. Quickspin changes playback speed only.
  • Autoplay starts the selected number of flights. Press the main stop button to stop autoplay after the current flight.
  • The bet is locked while a flight is running. During a single flight, the main stop button fast-forwards the current result.

Aircraft finishes

A gold finish may unlock through play. This is cosmetic and does not change flight results.
